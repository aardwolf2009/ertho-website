Learning & collaboration

Gouritz Cluster Biosphere Reserve

Shared knowledge.
Stronger conservancies.

Jacques co-facilitated a conservancy governance and strategy workshop across the GCBR landscape, alongside Apex Conservation Services and GCBR.

Explore the work +

ERTHO contributed governance and management guidance. Apex Conservation Services shared expertise in alien invasive plant management, while GCBR addressed participation in urban conservancies.

Attending conservancies and partner organisations received visual governance tools, a conservancy organogram and an interactive readiness self-assessment tool to support the discussion.

A constructive voice.
A collaborative
way forward.

Practical consulting

McGregor Vrolijkheid Conservancy

An objective response
to proposed development.

ERTHO helped the conservancy bring its mandate, technical information and stakeholder views together into a coherent public participation response.

Explore the work +

The work combined the conservancy’s adopted constitution, a desktop review of the pre-application draft scoping report, and comments from committee members and other stakeholders.

The submission recognised the prospective developer’s economic needs and private property rights, identified risks for consideration, and invited the developer to join the conservancy.

The approach kept voluntary cooperation at the centre of the conservancy’s response.

03 / Meet Jacques

Research informed.
Grounded in practice.

Jacques Smit

Founder, ERTHO (Pty) Ltd

Based in Robertson, Western Cape.
Working nationally. Open to international knowledge sharing and skills transfer.

Conservation depends on people as much as it depends on the places they care for.

Jacques brings experience across conservation management, eco-tourism, local government pollution control and organic certification auditing. His master’s research into Western Cape conservancies became the foundation for ERTHO’s approach to governance and management.

As a conservancy manager and chairperson of the Western Cape Conservation Stewardship Association, he understands the decisions, relationships and resource pressures that shape conservation work. ERTHO combines that experience with research, practical tools and access to a wider specialist network.
