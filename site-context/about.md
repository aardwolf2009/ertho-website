ERTHO works with collaborative private conservation initiatives across South Africa, focused on the governance and management structures that let conservation efforts survive and scale.

Company Background

ERTHO was founded by Jacques Smit, who works directly with conservancies, biosphere reserves, and conservation NGOs on the governance and management systems that let their initiatives last. His master's research examined governance and management across a sample of Western Cape conservancies. The adaptation of this research thesis, originally intended to be a standalone conservancy governance and management manual, gave rise to ERTHO (Pty) Ltd, which is a registered, tax compliant company offering diverse specialised environmental governance and management consulting services and products. ERTHO's recent work included co-facilitating a conservancy governance and strategy workshop for conservancies across the Gouritz Cluster Biosphere Reserve, drafting environmental impact public participation comments on behalf of a conservancy, facilitating committee meetings for a developing conservancy, and developing a methodology for the monitoring and evaluation of a provincial stewardship expansion project.

Founder profile

Jacques holds the degrees BSc Conservation Ecology (2009), PGDip Environmental Management (2021) and MPhil Environmental Management (2025). He has worked in various conservation and environmental management roles, ranging from eco-tourism, private sector conservation management and local government pollution control to private organic standard certification auditing. He is presently a conservancy manager and serves as chairperson of the Western Cape Conservation Stewardship Association. His career mirrors his passion for nature, so in his free time he enjoys relaxing in and exploring nature, whether on foot, by 4x4 or by mountain bike.
