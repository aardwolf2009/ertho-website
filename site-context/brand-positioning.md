# ERTHO — brand positioning and website brief

Updated from the client’s answers and supplied project example on 24 September 2026. This supersedes the earlier positioning draft. The service grouping below remains a working proposal for discussion.

## Positioning

**Practical expertise and sound governance for lasting conservation.**

ERTHO helps conservancies, private collaborative initiatives, conservation NGOs and regulators improve how conservation work is organised and carried out. It brings Jacques Smit’s specialist knowledge, practical experience and network to environmental challenges, organisational decisions and relationships between people.

Support combines clear advice, stronger governance, education and long-term strategic thinking. The aim is to help clients make informed decisions, work together constructively, and build the capability to sustain their conservation work.

## Audience and buying process

Clients include conservancies, private collaborative initiatives, conservation NGOs and regulators such as CapeNature. CapeNature is an example of the audience, not a confirmed named client endorsement.

Client organisations are typically small or new, often with little middle management. A chairperson, director or other senior person usually drives the engagement; a management committee commonly approves it. The approval process depends on the contracting organisation.

## Why clients seek ERTHO

- Jacques’s particular combination of conservation knowledge and practical experience.
- Education, advice, target setting and stronger organisational capability.
- An experienced perspective on regulatory processes and environmental problems, including development proposals and public participation.
- Help navigating sensitive relationships, negotiations and differing stakeholder interests.
- Access to a specialist network and thinking beyond the immediate problem.

ERTHO does not provide legal or financial advice. Regulatory experience should not be presented as enforcement authority. Budgeting and fundraising support should remain clearly described within this scope.

## Proposed service grouping

### 1. Practical consulting

Environmental challenges; public participation responses; technical and desktop document reviews; client advisory; facilitation and stakeholder dialogue.

### 2. Governance best practice

Roles and organisational structures; governance and management documents; procedures and position statements; planning and self-assessment; budgeting and fundraising strategy.

### 3. Learning & collaboration

Workshops and landowner days; education and skills transfer; practical tools; knowledge sharing; connections to appropriate specialists.

The third label is proposed, not client-approved. It gives education, participation and network access a clear place in the offer. These groups describe the help provided. Consulting projects and retainers describe how clients engage ERTHO; remote or in-person work describes delivery. Tools and templates can support all three groups.

## Commercial priorities

1. Consulting enquiries.
2. Ongoing retainers.

Use “Discuss your initiative” as the primary action. Present retainer support as a way to maintain access and continuity. No subscription pricing, guaranteed availability, membership entitlements or ready-to-buy product catalogue have been confirmed. The site invites enquiries about tools and templates rather than suggesting checkout or membership is available.

## Geography and language

Western Cape first, with national work. International information exchange and skills transfer are an aspiration and invitation to collaborate, not an established global operating footprint. Launch language: English.

## Evidence

- Jacques’s BSc Conservation Ecology (2009), PGDip Environmental Management (2021) and MPhil Environmental Management (2025), as recorded in the source About page.
- Research into Western Cape conservancies and practical management experience.
- GCBR conservancy governance and strategy workshop, co-facilitated alongside Apex Conservation Services and GCBR, supported by visual tools and a readiness self-assessment resource.
- McGregor Vrolijkheid Conservancy: integrated the adopted constitution, a technical desktop review of the pre-application draft scoping report, and committee/stakeholder comments into a constructive response. It recognised economic needs and property rights, identified risks and invited the prospective developer to join the conservancy. No later planning, development or ecological outcome has been supplied.

Do not invent testimonials, client counts, impact statistics, partnerships or endorsements.

## Voice and visual direction

Knowledgeable, practical, calm and collaborative. Translate governance into concrete responsibilities, decisions, plans and working relationships. Describe support without promising funding, regulatory approval or dispute resolution.

Use the supplied ERTHO logo, its yellow-green accent, warm off-white, deep green and earthy neutrals. The supplied service screenshot is a content reference, not a styling reference. Use ERTHO’s existing landscape and workshop photographs; do not imply stock imagery depicts a named project.

Working homepage expression: **Good intentions. Lasting conservation.**

## Website scope

A responsive single-page site built with semantic HTML, Tailwind CSS and simple JavaScript, prepared for GitHub Pages. Sections: introduction, audiences, support, engagement options, selected work, Jacques, resources and contact.

No payments, member login, gated content or enquiry form at launch. Direct email and WhatsApp are the contact paths. The free conservancy organogram is available as an image download; LinkedIn holds further public insights. The proposed service categories can be revised without changing the architecture.
