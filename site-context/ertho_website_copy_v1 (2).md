## 2. About

ERTHO (Pty) Ltd works with collaborative private conservation initiatives across South Africa, focused on the governance and management structures that let conservation efforts survive and scale.

ERTHO was founded by Jacques Smit, who works directly with conservancies, biosphere reserves, and conservation NGOs on the governance and management structures that let their initiatives last. His master's research examined governance and management across a sample of Western Cape conservancies, and recent work includes co-facilitating a conservancy governance and strategy workshop for conservancies across the Gouritz Cluster Biosphere Reserve (GCBR) landscape, alongside Apex Conservation Services and GCBR.

**Who we work with:**
- Young, developing private landowner conservancies
- Biosphere reserves
- NEMA Protected Environments
- Small conservation stewardship ENGOs

## 3. Services

- **Client advisory** — one-on-one governance and management guidance for your conservancy or initiative, including help responding to development applications and scoping reports that affect your area (note: ERTHO cannot and does not provide legal or financial advice)
- **Budgeting & fundraising strategy** — help drawing up a realistic budget linked to your management plan, and identifying and approaching appropriate funding sources
- **Workshops & landowner days** — practical sessions on conservancy governance and management fundamentals and adaptive co-management strategy. Past landowner day topics include birding, soil erosion control, conservation agriculture, botany, and counter-poaching, chosen around each conservancy's specific needs. Recent example: co-facilitated a governance and strategy workshop for conservancies across the Gouritz Cluster Biosphere Reserve (GCBR) landscape, alongside Apex Conservation Services and GCBR. ERTHO can help organise a landowner day end-to-end — duration, scheduling, topics, service providers, facilitators — on any budget.
- **Remote/desktop services** — desktop reviews of technical documents (e.g. environmental impact scoping reports), policy and procedural advice, and remote facilitation or demonstrations, without requiring an on-site visit
- **Digital products & templates** — ready-to-use governance templates (e.g. meeting agenda templates, self-assessment tools), sold individually or included with membership

## 6. Recent Work & Recommendations (public — builds credibility)

### GCBR Conservancy Governance & Strategy Workshop

Jacques Smit recently presented a conservancy governance and management talk and co-facilitated a conservancy strategy workshop for a group of conservancies within the Gouritz Cluster Biosphere Reserve (GCBR) landscape. ERTHO focused on conservancy governance and management fundamentals, while Apex Conservation Services gave a practical overview of alien invasive plant management, and GCBR added guidance on driving urban conservancy membership participation.

Conservancies often take on various functional environmental management responsibilities — alien invasive plant management being a prominent example — but doing so well requires an integrated, coherent approach. This is where sound governance and adaptive co-management come in. Conservancies have the potential to be powerhouses of conservation on members' private land, while also advocating for better environmental management beyond their boundaries, including on state land.

To support the in-person facilitation, ERTHO also provided attending conservancies and partner organisations with a set of digital tools: visual schematics for evaluating governance and management trajectory, a conservancy organogram, and an interactive Excel-based self-assessment tool for governance and management readiness.

*Think your conservancy or collaborative conservation initiative could benefit from workshops and tools like these? Get in touch.*

### Recommended: Join Conservation at Work

ERTHO strongly encourages conservancies to become members of Conservation at Work, the Western Cape Conservation Stewardship Association. Networking is a core part of good governance, and the organisation's annual two-day Symposium offers intensive knowledge-sharing and networking with peers and experts from the private, state, and NGO sectors.

Not yet a member? Reach out to Jacques Smit, any member of the Conservation at Work Executive Committee, or their administrative assistant at hello@conservationatwork.co.za to discuss the benefits.
